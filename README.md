A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes one monologue to `/output/entry.txt`.
Malachar waited nine hundred years for his eclipse — prophecy carved into the
mountain, letters taller than his castle — and on the night itself the scheme
drowns in administration: the moat needs dredging, the dredging needs a permit,
the permit needs the Dark Council, and the Council needs a quorum. The
tiredness lives in the pauses, the deflections, and a throne that already has
a meeting in it — never in a plain statement that he is weary.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), 107 words,
598 characters, inside the gate's 1–600 characters.
